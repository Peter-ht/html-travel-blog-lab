Adventures In Fire Island

My traveling experience to Tsavo West National Park was one of the best moments in my life.I was able to experience a moment of my life while seeing the migration of the wild beasts from Serengeti National park. Not only that i was able to view the tallest mountain in Africa Mount Kilimanjaro,where i could see the peak clearly because of the clouds. Some of the features that i liked about the park is watching the lions of the savannah resting after a meal under the acacia trees of the park.Also be able to go to the local hotel where you able to feed the giraffes from the comfort of your room.
