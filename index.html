Tsavo East National Park

It is well known for its savannah plains,stricking red volcanic dust earth and also the popular massive herds of the Red Elephants that covers themselves with the red soil which is rich in iron.

Tsavo West National Park

Famous for its mountanious topography with volcanic cones and lava flows.And also its big population in hippos and crocodile.

A land of wild animals and beauty

The land spans over 25,000 square kilometers,Tsavo is one of the largest game park in kenya and its also splitted into two;Tsavo East and Tsavo west where each of it has unique creatures and extinct wild animals.

Orphanage sanctuary

It is also second recognised orphanage for the wild animals that have been rescued from the wild.
